## Top five (impact ÷ effort)

1. **FR-01**: P0 at S effort, and the fix already exists. Every greenfield stranger hits a dead Continue button after the hardest 10 minutes of setup. Merging #104 removes it.
2. **FR-07**: P1 at S effort. It's a one-line default change, and the recommended provider currently undercuts the product's core promise (tailored letters) for everyone who follows the recommendation.
3. **FR-09**: P1 at S effort. The payoff is the trust receipt, and today it prints ✓ lines nothing verified. Tying each ✓ to a recorded check is small and protects every later support conversation.
4. **FR-03 + FR-04** (count as one lane): P1 at S effort. With a manual add on the empty board and in the URL modal's failure, a stranger has a way to track a job that doesn't depend on a worker. That's the missing half of the "tracked application" goal.
5. **FR-02**: P0 at M effort. It's the structural cause of the headline number, since no job can be tracked before about 22 minutes and three signups. Revealing the real board once B1 has a sheet turns "Poke around first" into a real way in.

## Not covered

- **Harness leak, reported for the orchestrator.** The in-process `dev-server.mjs` proxies same-origin `/profile/*` to `127.0.0.1:3847` (`dev-server.mjs:2341-2380`), which is Emilio's live API, and the fence lets same-origin requests through. My B3 "Draft from this text" (`s2-beats.mjs`) therefore POSTed a fictional four-line resume to the host's `/profile/from-resume`, and it drafted through the host's LLM pin. `~/.jobbored/resume.txt` and `profile.json` mtimes are unchanged (Sep 16), so nothing was written. After that I did not click B3 templates, B4 "Looks like me" (the fence 404s `POST /profile` anyway), or B5 "Save & verify". `/__proxy/discovery-env-key` would write into the host's discovery `.env`, and other auditors should avoid it too. Suggested harness fix: fulfill `/profile/*` and `/__proxy/discovery-env-key` in `installHermeticNetworkFence`.
- Not driven because the GIS stub never grants a token: the real Google consent screen, starter-sheet creation, and B1 completion in greenfield (FR-02's post-B1 demo-board behaviour is inferred from code). Nor B2 success, B5 SerpApi and Tailscale success, or a B6 run landing rows. All the timing for these steps is modelled, not measured.
- Beats 2–6 were reached with `goToBeat()` rather than earned. Their rendered state (for example the empty B4 draft) reflects that seam.
- Did not audit `?jb-v2=0` legacy onboarding, Settings re-entry ("Resume onboarding" menu, other than reading `auth-session.js:1025-1036`), or telemetry emission.
- Esc during an in-flight async step (B2 check, B5 worker restart) was not exercised.
