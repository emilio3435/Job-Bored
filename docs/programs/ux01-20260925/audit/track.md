## Method

- Harness: `docs/programs/ux01-20260925/audit/tools/audit-harness.mjs`, modes `signed-in` and `signed-in-empty` (plus `?jb-v2=0` for the legacy view), viewports 1440×900 and 375×812. The fence stayed in place, and Sheet writes went to the harness fixture's 200 stub.

  - `09-more-moves.mjs`: the dossier stepper move, and dragging to Applied then pressing Cancel.
  - `10-dawn.mjs`: the text of Dawn and Today in both modes.
  - `11-axe.mjs`: axe on today, dawn and pipeline, plus the number of Tab presses to reach the first card.
  - `12-card-signals.mjs`: how a board card shows an overdue follow-up, an owed reply, and an offer.
- In-flight check: none of the open PRs (#104–#121) touch Track surfaces. `bringup/pr114-kanban` (Rejected and Passed grid tracks) is already present in main's `pipeline.css`. So no finding below is fixed on another branch.

## Top five

1. **TR-06**: the Applied dialog discards what the user typed (P1, S). It is a one-call reroute through a planner that already writes date, follow-up and note, and it fixes the most important record in the tracker.
2. **TR-07**: the stage menu is painted under the next card (P1, S). It is the only keyboard and touch move path, and a stacking rule fixes it.
3. **TR-03**: a drag also opens the dossier and snaps the card back (P1, S). A one-flag fix removes a scroll jump, the double error toasts (TR-24) and the count corruption (TR-04).
4. **TR-01 with TR-02**: board moves write the Sheet but never update the app, and failures are swallowed (P0, M). Tracking itself reads wrong after every board move, so this has the highest impact, and one adapter change clears both.
5. **TR-08**: the board is unreadable at 375 (P0, M). No card can be read on a phone, and the stranger may be on any device.

The next candidates are TR-12 (Log follow-up never clears, S) and TR-15 (no "due soon" band, S).

## Not covered

- **Real Sheet round-trip.** The fixture always serves the original 12 rows and answers every write with 200, so I could not show that a reload repairs the TR-01 drift, or a real 401/403 rollback (TR-02 is from reading the code).
- **Touch drag.** Pointer drag was driven with a mouse. Touch-action and long-press behaviour on real phones were not tested.
- **Expired review modal.** With the fixture's data it has 0 candidates, so I did not drive the modal flow. Only the badge bug (TR-21) is covered.
- **Whats-next banner.** It is hidden in both signed-in modes and is setup-only rather than CRM, so I did not investigate why it stays hidden with infra complete.
- **Legacy view (`?jb-v2=0`).** I captured it (`audit/shots/track/legacy-view-full-1440.png`) but did not audit its interactions beyond the listener collision (TR-25).
- **The dossier's People block.** It is the only place to log a contact, a reply or a follow-up without the Sheet (answer to question 4: yes, through the Case's Contact, Last contact, Replied and Follow-up fields). I did not exercise its write paths; the dossier lens owns them.
- **Page-weight numbers.** Byte counts for the dormant renderers are file sizes on disk, not transfer sizes.
- **Harness safety stop (received after the report was written).** All of my runs took place between 05:29 and 05:40 CDT, which is before the harness patch. None of them pressed a save, verify, start, install, fix or draft control in discovery setup, Settings or onboarding. What they did click: board cards and a dossier open (twice per run at most), the stage menu, a drag, Today's "Start researching", "Log follow-up" and "Open and reply", the Applied dialog ("Mark submitted" and "Cancel"), and the dossier stage stepper. Every one of those Sheet writes went to the fixture's googleapis stub. Page load and dossier open may still have made same-origin reads (the materials index through `pipeline.js` `ensureMaterialsIndex`, and posting enrichment) that the old harness passed to the local server. I did not log same-origin traffic, so whether those reached `/__proxy/*` or `/profile/*` is unknown. The last run (`12-card-signals.mjs`) finished at 05:40:32, before the 05:41 worker restart.
