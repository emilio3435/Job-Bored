# The Case content-fit live check — 90 seconds

Run this immediately after discovery finishes, using the browser window size
you normally use with your real Sheet.

## 0–15 seconds — open the real role

1. Hard-reload JobBored and open the Pipeline.
2. Open **CSC Generation · Director, Lifecycle Marketing & CRM**.
3. Start at the top of The Case; keep normal zoom and the current window size.

## 15–40 seconds — check the first fold

1. Scroll from the navy role rail through the initial three-lane board.
2. Expect the rail, numbers, and the complete collapsed board within two screen
   heights. The shorter lanes end with their content; no lane leaves a long
   column of blank parchment.
3. Expect **They want**, **You have**, and **Your moves** to occupy the available
   columns without an empty fourth column.

## 40–65 seconds — inspect requirements

1. Expect no more than eight requirement bullets before the disclosure.
2. Expect a **Show all 25** button. Open it and confirm all 25 requirements
   appear.
3. Scan the expanded list and **You have**. Fail the check if any control token
   or fragment appears, especially `[<|`, `P&L management)`, the truncated
   `Proven omni-channel acumen (eCommerce` triplet, or a glued section header
   such as `Automation & Technology` / `Customer Intelligence & CDP` at the end
   of a requirement.

## 65–80 seconds — check evidence

1. Find a requirement marked **found** or **partial**.
2. Expect an evidence line directly under it: a sentence from the candidate
   profile plus its source (for example, `from your resume`).
3. Check one more marked requirement. A bare keyword such as `CRM`, `AI`,
   `API`, or `APIs` does not count as evidence.

## 80–90 seconds — check discovery date

1. In **The record**, find **Found**.
2. Expect its date to equal the local calendar day on which this discovery run
   completed—not tomorrow's UTC date.
3. Record PASS only if every check above held; otherwise capture the visible
   text and the failing section before closing the role.
