Product plan

Finish promises before adding surface area.

Feature lanes are sequenced by the repair gates they consume. Discovery, materials, and CRM work can then expand in parallel because they share stable event, evidence, and context contracts rather than competing state.

  1. “Now” completes functionality already visible or implied.
  2. “Next” adds direct weekly job-hunter leverage.
  3. “Later” establishes durable data needed for truthful learning.
  4. “Explore” stays behind evidence, privacy, and human-action boundaries.
  5. Prototype UI early; land data mutations only after their fix gate.
